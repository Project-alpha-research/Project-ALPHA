# Research-practice ideas from the owner's private research

**Status:** Idea
**Rough size:** M

## Problem / why

The owner's private research (October 2026 collection and the November handoffs) holds method
ideas that are not yet checklist rows. Restated in plain words, with no private text:

- A research queue with states: watch, investigate, waiting, rejected, revisit.
- Four kinds of uncertainty kept apart: facts, data, forecast, and the model's own answer; a
  language model's stated confidence is not a probability.
- Every claim labelled descriptive, predictive or causal; study modes named (reproduction,
  replication, adaptation, exploration, validation).
- An adapted strategy is a new hypothesis, not a replication; a chart observation is a
  hypothesis, not evidence.
- A transfer review per assumption (market, participants, data, regime, shorting, costs) when an
  idea moves from a paper to the owner's markets.
- A correction register: claims from earlier research that must not be repeated as general
  truths (a fixed backtest haircut, a parameter treated as an optimum, a new ledger treated as
  the full search history, a touched holdout treated as clean again).
- Combined signals never count duplicate inputs as independent evidence.
- A first worked exercise: one paper's source card and one labelled adaptation.
- If ALPHA were ever distributed, regulated-advice rules would need a legal review first; a
  disclaimer alone is not enough. Today it is private and single-owner.
- A research handoff never authorises live orders, paid spending or credential use.
- A fifth clock beside the four: the monitoring effort an idea needs from the owner.
- Crowding in four parts: the observed condition, the expected reaction, the actual reaction,
  and what would prove it wrong; positioning data carries its observation and release dates.
- Sources are checked for independence (many articles may repeat one origin), and each claim's
  source status (corrected, withdrawn) is kept.
- Result labels beside the study modes: reported, independently reproduced, modified, unverified.
- Seven minimal records: source, claim, study spec, data manifest, reference fixture, an
  append-only research event, and the decision.
- Worked ablations: value alone, with a quality score, with momentum, with both; rank buffers
  and sleeves compared at the same capital, overlapping orders netted before costs.
- Trend rules: tell acceleration from level, and a change of entry or exit from a change of
  size; a plain trend-following baseline only after the first frozen-rule milestone.
- Synthetic fixtures: a quote stamped 10:00:00 that arrives 10:00:02 cannot be used earlier; a
  crossed quote with fees nets to a loss; announcement lags on recession dates.
- Not to build: prop-challenge optimisation as a goal, high-frequency work, copying signal
  streams, a persona bot, exotic arbitrage.
- The owner wants the statistics behind a result explained in plain words, tied to the strategy.
- The private research names its own sources and their limits, and the material that would
  raise confidence if it were found; it lives outside git and is never committed.
