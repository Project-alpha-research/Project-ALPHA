# Research-partner and screen ideas recovered from earlier material

**Status:** Idea
**Rough size:** M

## Problem / why

Ideas from earlier reviews of other projects and earlier screen designs, restated in plain words.

- Record which sources an answer actually used, apart from sources that were only retrieved,
  including how many supplied items each run consumed.
- Check that a claimed number carries its unit, asset and interval, not only its value.
- A metric dictionary with conformance checks: Sharpe cadence, drawdown units and denominators,
  annual return, and whether a "backtest" means a forward markout or a portfolio result.
- Named investigation skills: loss and survival analysis, a data investigator for provider
  conflicts, a regime researcher, a pattern-protocol designer, a market-structure analyst, a
  forecast reviewer and a research critic.
- Screen patterns worth borrowing: drill-down by date and symbol with filter and export, and tasks
  grouped by purpose; every enabled action must reach a real result or an explicit unavailable state.
- One immutable explanation bundle that drives the screens, the HTML report and the PDF with exact
  parity, full-figure export, accessible equivalents, and optional animation after measurement.
- The owner's earlier terminal-style screen mock-ups (a market watch, a navigator, a job list,
  a report window, a governance window and several colour schemes) kept as a design reference.
- A prompt cheat sheet for the owner: if you mean this, name that layer, tool or screen.
- Other repositories reviewed in October (agent trading apps, a data platform, a news-analysis
  service, an execution toolkit, a desktop workbench, strategy script collections) are noted in the
  [outside projects register](../../reference/project/outside-projects.md).

## More recovered from the second pass

- A reading advisor: put whole books and papers into context instead of retrieving snippets, choose
  documents in tiers, check each claim against the source, and extract PDFs locally.
- What the owner wants, as design input: a systematic, data-tested edge in place of reacting to
  candles; private ideas over public strategies; the owner's chart reads turned into numbers; the
  human keeps the final call; every result explained in plain words with the short view first.
